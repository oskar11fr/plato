# Summarize a chapter

Write a page the reader can read in a few minutes to understand what this chapter says, how it fits into the book, and come back to later as a reference.

## Structure

Right under the title, write a **TL;DR** of two or three sentences: what the chapter does and its main point. Then use these sections, leaving out any that don't apply:

- `## Argument`: the chapter's main line of argument or explanation, step by step. Give key equations in LaTeX and say what each symbol means.
- `## Key points`: the claims, findings, examples or evidence the reader should remember, with numbers where the chapter gives them.
- `## Connections`: how the chapter builds on, revises or sets up other chapters, linking their pages. Leave this out for the first chapter unless it lays out the plan of the book.
- `## Key concepts`: the concepts introduced or used centrally here, one per line, each as a wiki link with a one-line definition.
- `## Open questions`: only if the chapter raises some worth following up.

For an introduction, preface or conclusion, adapt the sections: say what the book sets out to do, or what it concludes.

## Accuracy

- State only what the book says. If something is unclear or missing, say so rather than filling the gap from general knowledge. If background knowledge is needed to follow the page, mark it as background.
- Cite the page for every specific claim, number and equation.
- Report numbers exactly as given, with their units and setting.
- Quote the book's exact wording only for definitions and central claims.
- Describe earlier chapters only as their TL;DRs describe them; don't guess at what they contain.

## Style

- Plain, direct sentences. Explain terms on first use, unless an earlier chapter's page already did.
- Aim for 300 to 700 words; go longer only for a long, dense chapter.
- No filler such as "this chapter offers an interesting perspective".

## The reader's input

- If the reader names a focus for the book, give that part more depth and keep the rest brief.
- If the reader highlighted passages in this chapter, make sure the page covers what they say.
